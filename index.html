Hello, I’m Na Hyeon Park.
I started in design and have grown into planning and project management. I love seeing an idea become a finished piece, and making it together with people along the way. Rather than staying within one field, I keep looking for ways to create better experiences.

To me, good design leaves a scene that stays with you: a memory held in an object, the warmth of a quiet moment, a small discovery. So before deciding what to show, I think about how it will be received.

All work is work with people. I draw energy and inspiration from those I work with, and carry it back into the work. Building relationships where good influence flows between brands, people, and those who make things together: that’s why I keep doing design and planning.
